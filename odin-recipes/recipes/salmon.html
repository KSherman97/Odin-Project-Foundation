Baked Salmon

Flaky salmon fillets baked to perfection with lemon and herbs. This easy and healthy recipe is ideal for a quick and satisfying meal for two.


Ingredients

  • 2 (6 ounce / 170g each) salmon fillets, skin on or off
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 lemon, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried dill
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt, or to taste
  • 1/8 teaspoon black pepper, or to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400
  2. Line a baking sheet with parchement paper
  3. Pat the salmon fillets dry with paper towels
  4. Place the salmon fillets on the prepared baking sheet
  5. Drizzle 1 tablespoon olive oil over the salmon
  6. Sprikle 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder, 1/2 teaspoon dill, 1/4 teaspoon salt, and 1/8 teaspoon black pepper
  7. Arrange 1/2 lemon over the salmon
  8. Bake the salmon for 12-15 minutes, or until the salmon is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork. Fish is done at 145 degrees
  9. Remove from oven and serve immediantely. Use extra lemon wedges on the side for squeezing over the fish
