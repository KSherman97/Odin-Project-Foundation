Cheese Pizza

Cheese pizza is a classic Italian dish. It consists of a tomato sauce base on top of a hand tossed pizza dough crust, and topped with a perfectly hydrated cheese blend. In the world of pizza, hydration is everything.

Ingredients

  • Hand Tossed Pizza Crust
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 500
  2. Let dough warm up to about 60 degrees
  3. Place flour on the table you will stretch the dough to prevent sticking
  4. stretch dough into a nice circle and place on a dough screen
  5. sauce the dough in a circular motion slowly creating rings further from the center
  6. Top with a reasonable amount of cheese without using too much or too little
