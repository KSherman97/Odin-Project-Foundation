Recipe - Lasagna

Lasanga

Lasanga is a wonderful Italian dish. Lasagna typically consists of a tomato sauce base, long and thin noodles, beef, and some form of cheese.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna Noodle
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Beef (or any protein)
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350
  2. Place noodles down in layers with sauce and meat between
  3. Top with cheese
  4. Cook for 30 minutes and remove when cheese is slightly bubbled
